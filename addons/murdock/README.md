# Home Assistant Add-on: Murdock

Speaker recognition proxy for Home Assistant voice pipelines.

[![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]][amd64]
[![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield]][aarch64]

Sits between your Wyoming satellites and your STT engine, transparently
forwarding transcription traffic while identifying *who* is speaking
and pushing the result to HA entities your automations can trigger on.

See [`DOCS.md`](DOCS.md) for full installation, configuration, and
usage instructions.
