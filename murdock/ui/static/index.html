Voice map (embedding space)

2-D projection (PCA) of every sample embedding: one color per speaker, large rings are centroids, gray crosses are untagged unknown samples. Tight, well-separated clusters mean reliable recognition; samples drifting toward another cluster are candidates for pruning.

Whisper detection (experimental)

Flags utterances that were whispered, so your conversation agent can answer quietly. Whispering has no vocal-fold vibration, which is a far clearer signal than most voice properties — but it also wrecks speaker recognition, so a whispered command will usually come back as “unknown”. That is deliberate: treating a whisper as proof of identity would turn this into a way past the gate.

While this is on, the TV/noise liveness gate and early reject are skipped for utterances detected as whispered — otherwise they would throw them away, because a whisper looks quiet and flat to those heuristics.

Per-satellite thresholds

Override the verify threshold for individual satellites (e.g. a noisy kitchen speaker vs. a quiet study). Leave empty to fall back to the global setting above.

Confidence calibration

Maps the raw cosine distance to a calibrated probability (Platt scaling) so the confidence reported to Home Assistant is meaningful. Fitted automatically from your enrolled samples; gating still uses the distance threshold.

Sample quality (advanced)

Adjust how sample quality is scored. Scores drive the smart auto-enroll replacement logic (worst sample is only replaced when a better one comes in) and the per-speaker training quality indicator. Weights are normalized automatically.
